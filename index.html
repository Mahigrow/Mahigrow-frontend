Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

Each product has its own MOQ — typically 1 carton (e.g. 12 bottles, 10 bags, 50 sachets). The MOQ and carton step are shown on each product card. Tier pricing activates automatically as your quantity increases.

How does bulk tier pricing work?

Every product has 3 quantity tiers. The more you order, the lower the per-unit price. The active tier is shown on the product card and updates live as you adjust quantity. For example: 12–47 bottles at ₹420, 48–119 at ₹395, 120+ at ₹368.

How fast will my order be delivered?

Orders placed before 5 PM are dispatched same day. Delivery takes 2–4 working days across AP & Telangana. You'll receive a tracking link on WhatsApp and SMS once dispatched.

Do I get a GST invoice?

Yes. A GST-compliant purchase invoice is generated automatically after payment and sent to your registered email. Ensure your GSTIN is entered correctly at registration.

Can I order via WhatsApp?

Yes. Click "Order via WhatsApp" in the order drawer — it pre-fills your full order details and sends them to our team. We'll send a Razorpay payment link within 30 minutes.
