Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

Each product has its own MOQ — typically 1 carton (e.g. 12 bottles, 10 bags, 50 sachets). MOQ and the carton step are shown on each product card. Tier pricing activates automatically as quantity increases.

How does bulk tier pricing work?

Every product has 3 quantity tiers. The more you order, the lower the per-unit price. The active tier updates live as you adjust quantity.

How fast is delivery?

Orders placed before 5 PM are dispatched same day. Delivery takes 2–4 working days. You'll receive a tracking link on WhatsApp once dispatched.

Do I get a GST invoice?

Yes. A GST-compliant purchase invoice is generated after payment and sent to your registered email.

Can I order via WhatsApp?

Yes. Click "Order via WhatsApp" in the order drawer — it pre-fills your order details and sends them to our team. We'll send a Razorpay payment link within 30 minutes.
